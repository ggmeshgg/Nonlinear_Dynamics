FIELD NOTES

01

Locked is not identical

A locked firefly runs at the beacon frequency, but it can follow with a constant phase lag.

02

Near the edge

Outside the locking band, long almost-locked pauses end in sudden full-cycle phase slips.

03

This is external entrainment

Every firefly follows one beacon. Mutual firefly-to-firefly coupling requires a different model.
